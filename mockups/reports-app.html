Muse Reports app — how it works
  • Own installable page (reports.html), same pattern as Muse Staff — synced live to the salon.
  • Login: front-desk PIN. Only accounts whose role has View Reports & Payroll get in (admins always). Others see "no access."
  • Reports view: range chips (Today / Yesterday / This week / Pay period / This month), gross-income hero card, payment mix with ticket counts, by-staff and by-service breakdowns, fees/discounts/gift-card lines, and the transaction list.
  • Payroll view: ‹ › pay-period nav; per-tech cards (billed · commission · check · cash · total, 🔒 badge when locked) + the Front Desk hourly table — same numbers as the dashboard (overrides + locks honored).
  • Read-only: nothing on this page can change data.
