Calendar — Week view

A Day | Week toggle joins the date nav. Week view is the overview: 7 day columns, every visible tech's appointments merged per day and colored by tech (same colors as the day columns). A party that spans techs shows as ONE block. Tap a day header (or any empty space in a column) to jump into that day's normal Day view; tap a block for the usual appointment popover. ←/→ move a whole week.

  • Block color = tech (their Google-calendar color, same as day view). “Calendars” show/hide filtering applies here too.
  • One block per booking — a multi-tech party is deduped and shown once, colored by the primary tech.
  • Tap a day header / empty space → that day in Day view. Tap a block → the normal appointment popover (check-in, confirm, edit…).
  • New Appointment works as today (defaults to the viewed week's day after drill-in). Creating by tapping a slot stays a Day-view gesture.
  • The red “now” line shows in today's column; the visible-hours setting (muse_cal_hours) and Ctrl+wheel zoom apply unchanged.
  • View choice is remembered per device (muse_cal_view).
